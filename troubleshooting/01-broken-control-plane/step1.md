# 🐉 Troubleshoot the Incident

A production Kubernetes environment is currently experiencing an outage.

You have administrative access to the control-plane node.

Something is wrong with the cluster.

## Your task

Investigate the environment and restore normal operation.

You should determine:

- Why Kubernetes API operations are failing.
- Why a workload cannot be scheduled.
- What is preventing the cluster from operating normally.
- How to safely restore the affected components.

Do not rebuild the cluster.

Do not use destructive shortcuts.

## Success

The challenge is complete when the final validation passes.

You are expected to investigate the problem yourself.

There are no individual hints in this step.
